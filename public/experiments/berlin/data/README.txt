Building footprints and wall heights throughout the Ringbahn interior include Berlin official LoD2 data.
https://gdi.berlin.de/data/a_lod2/ — Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero 2.0.
See mitte-buildings.json and ringbahn-buildings.json for source tiles, conversion method and coverage.

hochbahn.json contains the elevated U1/U3 track centre lines and station platform outlines,
derived from OpenStreetMap route relations (ODbL 1.0). It includes source URLs and hashes.
Structural sections, canopies and the vertical profile are simplified visual approximations.
