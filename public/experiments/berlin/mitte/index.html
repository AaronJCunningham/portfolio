What’s real here?

The official layer preserves CityGML wall, roof and courtyard surfaces. LoD2 roofs are generalized. This data has no photographic façades, windows or business signs.

Road positions come from OpenStreetMap. Ground between buildings is interpolated from their bases. The previous layer shows plain extrusions of the simulator’s existing footprints and heights.

Berlin’s textured aerial mesh would be the next appearance test. Its download portal was under maintenance when this trial was prepared.
